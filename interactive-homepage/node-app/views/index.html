🚀 SolarChaos

Predicting Satellite Lifecycles & Risks with Cosmic Insight

📖 Overview

SolarChaos uses machine learning and orbital dynamics to predict satellite lifecycle, deorbit events, and solar weather interactions with cosmic precision.

🧠 Features

  • 📆 Mission Timeline: Predicts end-of-life
  • 🌍 Deorbit Path & Re-entry Simulation
  • 🌞 Solar Storm Risk Forecast
  • 🛰️ Debris Collision Analyzer
