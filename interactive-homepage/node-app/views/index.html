🚀 SolarChaos

Predicting Satellite Lifecycles & Risks with Cosmic Insight

📖 Overview

SolarChaos is a Streamlit-powered dashboard that predicts the lifecycle and risk profile of satellites based on launch parameters. It helps researchers, engineers, and space enthusiasts assess mission viability and environmental threats in orbit.

🧠 Features

  • 📆 Mission Timeline: Calculates satellite age and end-of-life
  • 🌍 Deorbit Location Predictor: Simulates re-entry location
  • 🌞 Solar Storm Risk Forecaster: Flags solar cycle overlaps
  • 🗑️ Debris Collision Analyzer: Assesses collision risk

SolarChaos © 2025 | Built with Node.js & Streamlit | Designed for space research and mission planning
